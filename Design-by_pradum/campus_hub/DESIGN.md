## Brand & Style

This design system translates the communal vitality of an academic campus into an exacting editorial publication. Inspired by archival broadsheets, varsity directories, and structured index registries, the aesthetic fuses the warmth of tactile print with the utility of modern high-density productivity tools.

### Personality & Core Attributes
- **Authoritative & Scholarly:** Grounded in collegiate publishing conventions with strong typographic anchors and structured column work.
- **Tactile Precision:** Physical broadsheet and noticeboard references executed through crisp ink, warm paper textures, and hairline dividers.
- **Utilitarian & Legible:** High scanability engineered for rapid campus notices, hub registries, schedules, and alerts without decorative noise.

### Stylistic Constraints
- **Strictly No Gradients or Blurs:** Flat planes exclusively. Avoid blurred backdrops, glassmorphism, decorative glow blobs, and drop-shadow fuzz.
- **Graphic Authenticity:** Eschew generic stock illustrations and standard emoji sets. Use typographic accents, strict geometric rule dividers, ink stamps, and high-contrast monospaced metadata markers.

## Colors

The palette operates on an ink-on-paper philosophy, counterbalanced by traditional institutional accent tones and hub-specific taxonomy tints.

### Foundation Tones
- **Paper Canvas (`#F5F1E8`):** The unbleached warm background representing newsprint and heavy archival stock.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFDF9`):** Pure elevated paper layer reserved for active notice cards, forms, and reading blocks.
- **Sunken Surface (`#EDE7DA`):** Indented structural regions, card troughs, and table heads.
- **Ink Line (`#DDD6C7`):** Hairline boundary color for tabular grids, section rules, and index tabs.

### Typography Hierarchy
- **Ink Primary (`#1A1A17`):** Maximum contrast reading ink for titles, lead copy, and interactive buttons.
- **Ink Secondary (`#57534A`):** Descriptive metadata, subtitles, and secondary card bodies.
- **Ink Muted (`#8C867A`):** De-emphasized timestamps, disabled inputs, and column guides.

### Accents & Functional Coding
- **Oxblood (`#7B1E2E` / Tint `#F3E3E3`):** Academic institutional seal color for critical announcements, identity badges, and university alerts.
- **Marigold (`#F2B33D`):** Editorial highlighter yellow for active focus states, sticky pins, and urgent visual cues.

### Hub Taxonomy Channels
- **Hub 01 (Lost & Found):** `#A0660E` (Surface Tint: `#FBF0D9`)
- **Hub 02 (Sports):** `#2E6B4E` (Surface Tint: `#E2EFE7`)
- **Hub 03 (Booking Board):** `#2C5A8C` (Surface Tint: `#E1EAF4`)
- **Hub 04 (Market):** `#B4532A` (Surface Tint: `#F7E6DC`)
- **Hub 05 (Emergency):** `#D7261E` (Surface Tint: `#FCE4E2`)
- **Hub 06 (Feedback):** `#1E6E6A` (Surface Tint: `#DDEFED`)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial, sturdy cadence of Domine with the functional, unpretentious clarity of Hanken Grotesk.

### Pairing Strategy
- **Domine:** Commands section headers, notice titles, campus mastheads, and high-level card headlines. Its bracketed serifs convey authority and literary heritage.
- **Hanken Grotesk:** Drives reading copy, tabular data, form controls, and micro-labels. Maintains clean geometry and legibility across dense dashboard modules.
- **Eyebrow Treatments:** Uppercase labels, taxonomy stamps, and status badges utilize `eyebrow` styling in Hanken Grotesk at `0.08em` tracking to emulate stamped ledger metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural grid inspired by folded newspaper columns and indexing ledgers. 

### Grid System
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 12-column dynamic framework with `gutter-lg` (32px) and `margin-lg` (48px). Cards align strictly to column increments.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 8-column system with `gutter` (20px) and `margin` (24px) for dashboard grids and list feeds.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 4-column column system with `gutter-sm` (12px) and `margin-sm` (16px). Secondary modules collapse beneath primary bulletins.

### Spatial Rhythm
Layout uses a disciplined 4px/8px baseline rhythm. Vertical gaps between modules emphasize white space as unprinted paper, using hairline grid borders (`#DDD6C7`) to enforce structure rather than spatial emptiness.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established strictly through architectural surface stacking and ruled line boundaries.

### Flat Card & Border Architecture
- **Elevation 0 (Background):** Base paper layer (`#F5F1E8`).
- **Elevation 1 (Document Surface):** Elevated cards (`#FFFDF9`) framed by a continuous `1px solid #DDD6C7` outline. Absolutely zero drop shadows or box blurs.
- **Elevation 2 (Floating Modals / Pinned Flyers):** Surface `#FFFDF9` with an intentional double-border simulation or high-contrast crisp offset outline (`2px solid #1A1A17`).
- **Sunken Trough:** Used for search trays, secondary metadata bars, and inactive tabs via `#EDE7DA`.

### Contrast Anchors
Visual weight is communicated through typographic scale, ink density, and hub color flags rather than faux z-axis elevation.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, structured, and deliberate curvature (`roundedness: 1`), evoking crisp, die-cut index cards and trimmed rag paper.

### Corner Archetypes
- **Standard Corners (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Form controls, small badges, and tooltips.
- **Containers & Index Cards (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Primary notice cards, sheet containers, and search bars.
- **Outer Shells & Mastheads (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Canvas wrapper panels, major module sections, and dialog viewports.
- **Notched Index Tabs:** Chamfered or top-rounded-only tabs simulating tangible cardboard dividers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1A1A17` fill with `#F5F1E8` text. Height is 44px with a 4px to 8px corner radius. No shadow. Active state shifts background to `#57534A`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** `#FFFDF9` surface with a `1px solid #DDD6C7` boundary and `#1A1A17` typography. Hover shifts background to `#EDE7DA`.
- **Destructive / Oxblood:** Solid `#7B1E2E` fill with `#FFFDF9` text. Focus ring transitions to `#F2B33D`.

### Cards & Noticeboard Tiles
- Background `#FFFDF9`, border `1px solid #DDD6C7`, padding `1.5rem`.
- Structural headers contain a category eyebrow in `eyebrow` typography with hub-specific tint pills.
- Dividing rules inside cards utilize `1px solid #DDD6C7` with no margin collapse.

### Index Tabs & Segmented Controls
- Positioned atop containers with top-radius rounding and sharp square bottoms.
- Inactive tabs blend into `#EDE7DA` with muted ink. Active tab lifts into `#FFFDF9` with a solid `2px` top border in `#1A1A17` or relevant Hub accent.

### Text Inputs & Form Fields
- Fixed 44px height on interactive inputs.
- Background `#FFFDF9`, border `1px solid #DDD6C7`, 4px radius. Placeholder text in `#8C867A`.
- **Focus State:** Replaces default browser ring with a crisp `2px solid #1A1A17` border paired with an external `2px solid #F2B33D` highlight halo.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact 24px height, font size `11px`, tracking `0.08em`, uppercase.
- Constructed using the hub tint as background with the corresponding solid tone for typography and a hairline border.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp `1.25rem` squares with a `1.5px solid #57534A` outline.
- Checked state fills with `#1A1A17` featuring a pure paper `#F5F1E8` tick mark. Radios remain strictly circular with an internal ink dot.
