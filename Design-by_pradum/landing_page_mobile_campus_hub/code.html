COMMON QUERIES

Questions

Everything you need to know about the platform guidelines and access.

Who can use it? +
Only SIT Nagpur students, faculty, and verified campus staff with active institutional credentials.
Is it free? +
Yes, 100% free with no commercial advertisements or paid tiers.
How many slots can I book? +
To ensure fair access across the student body, each student can hold a maximum of 2 slots per day.
Is anonymous feedback really anonymous? +
Yes, your name, PRN, and profile are never stored or shown on anonymous feedback submissions.
Who manages emergency contacts? +
The college administration and security office manage and verify all contact lines weekly.
